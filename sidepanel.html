Durable storage

Browser-managed thumbnail storage is the default. You may also choose a normal local folder so generated WebP thumbnails survive removing or reinstalling FileChute.

Keep metadata outside Chromium too?

FileChute always keeps a fast browser-local copy. You can also choose a normal folder for a durable filechute-metadata.json mirror, so provenance and future tags survive browser-data cleanup.

Keep thumbnails outside Chromium too?

Generated image and video thumbnails are cached in the browser for speed. FileChute can also mirror the tiny WebP previews into a folder you choose so they survive browser cache, extension-data cleanup, or removing FileChute.
