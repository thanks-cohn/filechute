Durable storage

Keep metadata outside Chromium too?

FileChute always keeps a fast browser-local copy. You can also choose a normal folder for a durable filechute-metadata.json mirror, so provenance and future tags survive browser-data cleanup.

Keep thumbnails outside Chromium too?

Generated image and video thumbnails are cached in the browser for speed. FileChute can also mirror the tiny WebP previews into a folder you choose so they survive browser cache or extension-data cleanup.
